# GCT2 and BCO1 — design handoff

**The approved plan is now specified in two separate protocols. No new GPU study has been launched or registered.**

## GCT2: confirm the pretrained-growth result

Six new independent six-layer parents, the identical GCT1 six-to-twelve stacking/moment-transfer operation, and eighteen three-arm continuations. Retain all training settings, the matched-direction construction and symmetric scientific instrumentation. Pilot parents 159–160 are excluded. Candidate IDs 161–166 await a complete live collision audit and registration; no silent seed replacement.

B-good receives 161/163/165 and B-default 162/164/166, both on S1-B. Arm order is counterbalanced within each lane and uses all six permutations over the cohort. B-default admits work only after GSR1 finishes and normal resource/process checks pass. No cross-account cache substitution is planned.

All eighteen final checkpoints are locked before REPORT evaluation on the established offset-2,500,000 partition. The two paired comparisons are BDT–matched and BDT–Adam; each receives ordinary 95% and family-adjusted 97.5% t intervals. Joint confirmation requires both adjusted upper bounds below zero and valid execution checks. Six is a fixed planning cohort, not a power guarantee. No pooling of pilot parents, optional stopping, extra parents or recipe changes.

Scientific work is 97,380 mixed-size optimizer updates, split equally across the two lanes. Planning calls for 128 GiB free shared disk and 32 GiB free GPU memory per admitted child; these are guards, not reservations.

The recovery design now namespaces gate registrations by lane/GPU/runtime/source, and validates completed jobs before opening a child log. This avoids both the previous GPU-migration registry collision and the empty-log burst during successful skips.

## BCO1: one cost-reduction pass

First profile the original lean canonical BDT, with ordinary and every-tenth-step refresh updates separated. Then evaluate one candidate that avoids two optional reporting reductions when diagnostics are not requested. It preserves output arithmetic, covariance updates, finite/order validation, and the condition/norm fields required by the production safety guard.

The small-stack CPU check matched proposals and covariance/eigen/counter state through 1,000 FP32 updates and all registered edge-case scenarios. This is not a full-model/CUDA equivalence result or a speed result.

The next engineering gates use a shared update-200 parent and three reference/candidate paths through update 1,000, including checkpoint interruption at 310. Only exact agreement permits measurement. A new paired timing study uses split Adam, reference BDT and candidate BDT in twelve balanced blocks per family, with the OCB1 timing windows, common-state restoration and sentinels.

The advancement screen is at least 2% median full-step improvement, faster in at least ten of twelve blocks, sufficient sentinel stability and all exact gates passed. Halving the remaining overhead is an aspiration, not a scientific acceptance criterion. The initial allocation ends after this one candidate/measurement cycle. GCT2 never switches to the engineering candidate mid-study.
